About the data

A note on the numbers.

Small areas. Important uncertainty.

These are survey estimates, not exact counts. Select an area to see its margin of error. Differences in color do not establish statistically significant differences.

Sources: City of Chicago, community areas, and Census TIGER/Line. Rent and housing counts: ACS 2010–2014, 2015–2019, and 2020–2024 (B25058, B25064, B25063, B25003, B25070, B25042, B25069), matched to each release’s TIGER boundaries. Rents are in each period’s end-year dollars, without adjustment for inflation. Boundaries can change between periods. Colors show estimates, not statistical significance. Whole block groups and tracts with positive city overlap are shown; some extend beyond city limits.

Reading margins of error

The ± amount next to an estimate is its Census-published margin of error at the 90% confidence level. Chart error bars show the estimate plus or minus that amount. These describe sampling uncertainty; they do not include all possible sources of error.

Rent burden, bedrooms, and utilities

Rent-burden measures show the share spending 30% or more, or 50% or more, of household income on gross rent. Their denominator excludes units for which the rent/income ratio could not be computed. A separate not-computed share uses all renter-occupied units.

Bedroom shares use all renter-occupied units, including homes occupied without cash rent. Studios have no separate bedroom. The three-plus-bedroom share overlaps the three-, four-, and five-plus-bedroom shares.

Utilities measures show the shares of all renter-occupied units paying extra for one or more utilities, or paying no extra for any utilities. They do not estimate utility spending.

Percentage margins of error are approximate and shown in percentage points (pp), using Census-style formulas for sums and subset proportions. Missing inputs and zero denominators remain unavailable, rather than becoming zero percentages. Chart error bars may extend above 100% under these approximations. Citywide percentages are calculated from citywide counts, not by averaging mapped areas.
