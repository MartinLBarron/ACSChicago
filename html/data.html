About the data

A note on the numbers.

Small areas. Important uncertainty.

These are survey estimates, not exact counts. Select an area to see its margin of error. Differences in color do not establish statistically significant differences.

Sources: City of Chicago, community areas, and Census TIGER/Line. Rent and housing counts: ACS 2010–2014, 2015–2019, and 2020–2024 (B25058, B25064, B25063, B25003), matched to each release’s TIGER boundaries. Rents are in each period’s end-year dollars, without adjustment for inflation. Boundaries can change between periods. Colors show estimates, not statistical significance. Whole block groups and tracts with positive city overlap are shown; some extend beyond city limits.
