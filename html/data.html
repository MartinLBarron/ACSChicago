About the data

A note on the numbers.

Geographic Inclusion

The current model retains whole block groups with at least half their area inside Chicago and complete usable model inputs. The dashboard uses a broader positive-overlap selection. ACS source estimates, margins of error and annotations are preserved; derived percentage margins of error are approximate.

Reading margins of error

ACS data are survey estimates, not exact counts. Select an area to see its margin of error. Differences in color do not establish statistically significant differences.

The ± amount next to a direct ACS estimate is its Census-published margin of error at the 90% confidence level. Derived percentages use approximate margins of error calculated from the source counts. These describe sampling uncertainty; they do not include all possible sources of error.

Variable guide

All dashboard measures and current model variables are listed below. Original ACS table names and cell numbers identify the source counts or estimates. Year links open the Census API variable dictionary for each release, where estimate (E) and margin-of-error (M) fields are documented. “View estimates” opens the corresponding 2024 table on data.census.gov, where you can select a geography.

Cell formulas below use the linked table prefix: for example, B25003 cell 003 means B25003_003E for the estimate and B25003_003M for its margin of error. A range means the sum of those cells. Every derived share is multiplied by 100. Cell numbers refer to 2024 unless a release-specific difference is noted.
